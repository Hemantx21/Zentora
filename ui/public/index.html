Our Mission

Zentora empowers freelancers and clients to collaborate seamlessly — from posting projects and placing bids to finalizing deals with full transparency. We believe great work happens when the right talent finds the right opportunity.

Whether you're a startup hiring your first developer or a seasoned freelancer building your portfolio, Zentora gives you the tools to connect, collaborate, and earn — all in INR with credit-based bidding built for the Indian market.

For Clients

Post projects with clear budgets and timelines. Review competitive bids from verified freelancers. Select the best match and finalize deals — all from your dashboard.

For Freelancers

Browse live projects, place bids using credits, and grow your earnings. Choose a plan that fits your workflow — from free Starter to Elite with 200 credits.

Project Posting

Clients post projects with title, description, budget (₹), and timeline. No category hassle — just clear requirements.

Credit-Based Bidding

Freelancers use credits to place bids. 1 credit = 1 bid. Plans from free Starter (10 credits) to Elite (200 credits).

Deal Finalization

Clients review all bids, select a freelancer, and finalize the deal. Other bids are automatically rejected.

Escrow & Payments

Secure escrow monitoring for accepted bids. Track platform volume and accepted bid values in real time.

Profile Management

Update photo, contact info, bio, skills, and hourly rate. Separate flows for Admin, Client, and Freelancer.

Admin Dashboard

Manage users, clients, projects, credit plans, and bids. Full platform oversight from one central panel.

How Credits Work

Every bid you place costs 1 credit. When your credits run out, upgrade your plan or wait for your next billing cycle. Clients post projects for free — no subscription required.
